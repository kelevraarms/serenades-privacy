# Privacy Policy for Serene Den

**Last updated:** September 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how your information is collected, used, and disclosed when you use the mobile application **Serene Den** ("the App").

### 1. Information Collection and Use
Serene Den is designed as an inspirational and mindful quote tool. The App does not require user registration and does not collect or transmit personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise physical location) to our servers.

### 2. Third-Party Services and Advertising
The App uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device and serve advertisements.

**Google AdMob**
We use Google AdMob to display advertisements in the App. AdMob may automatically collect certain device data and identifiers to serve relevant ads, monitor fraud, and perform analytics. This data may include:
- IP address
- Device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / AAID)
- Diagnostic, crash, and ad performance logs

For more details on how Google processes and safeguards data, please review:
- [Google Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

### 3. Local Device Storage
The App may use local storage on your device (such as Preferences or IndexedDB) exclusively to save your favorite quotes, bookmarks, or display settings locally. This information never leaves your device.

### 4. Children’s Privacy
The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

### 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
